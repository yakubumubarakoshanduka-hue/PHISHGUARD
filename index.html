          Dataset & setup

          11,055 instances (UCI, PhishTank, Alexa, Cisco Umbrella), 10,890 after removing 165 duplicates; 30 → 20 features, top 15 selected with a correlation filter and RFE. 80/20 stratified split; GridSearchCV with 5-fold CV; best settings: 100 trees, unlimited depth, max_features = sqrt, class_weight = balanced. The forest used on the Scan page is a 51-tree demonstration ensemble built from these 15 features and importance scores, not the Jupyter-trained model.
